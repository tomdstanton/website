# SideroScanner

**SideroScanner** is a specialized tool for annotating TonB-dependent iron-regulated outer membrane proteins (IROMPs) and siderophore receptors in Gram-negative bacteria.

Iron-uptake receptors are critical entry portals for siderophore-cephalosporin conjugates such as **cefiderocol**, as well as key virulence factors in pathogens like *Klebsiella pneumoniae*, *Acinetobacter baumannii*, and *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.

## Highlights

- **Targeted Receptor Profiling:** Detects and classifies homologous siderophore receptors across diverse Gram-negative assemblies.
- **Mutation & Truncation Screening:** Helps link structural disruptions or frameshifts in iron transport pathways to reduced cefiderocol susceptibility.
